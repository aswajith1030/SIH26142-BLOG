Project Overview

What is DeepSRM-AI?

DeepSRM-AI is an AI-based Super-Resolution Mapping framework that learns the relationship between medium-resolution Sentinel-2 imagery and high-resolution reference data to estimate fine-scale spatial information.

Enhanced Multispectral Imagery

Reconstructs high-frequency spatial details across Sentinel-2's 10m and 20m spectral bands while preserving physical radiometric surface reflectance ratios.

Fine-Resolution Semantic Maps

Generates high-precision sub-pixel land-cover classifications, separating crops, buildings, roads, soil, and water boundaries that are mixed in 10m input pixels.

Confidence & Uncertainty Data

Quantifies spatial prediction confidence at every subpixel location, providing essential validation metrics for mission-critical decision-making.

Remote Sensing Bottlenecks

The Challenge

Current limitations in freely available satellite earth observation imagery constrain localized decision-making.

01

Limited Spatial Resolution

10m Sentinel-2 imagery does not provide sufficient spatial detail for fine-scale analysis.

02

Mixed Pixels

A single coarse pixel may contain multiple land-cover classes such as crops, roads, buildings, soil and water.

03

Missing Fine Features

Small buildings, narrow roads, field boundaries and localized damage may remain unclear.

04

Reduced Analytical Utility

Limited spatial detail can affect land-cover mapping, change detection, agriculture monitoring and disaster assessment.

Uncertain Reconstruction

AI-generated fine details may be model-inferred and therefore require validation.

STEP 01: Sentinel-2 Input Satellite Imagery

Multispectral Input Acquisition
Interactive Stage

DeepSRM-AI ingests freely accessible 10m Sentinel-2 satellite imagery containing 13 spectral bands covering optical, near-infrared (NIR), and short-wave infrared (SWIR) spectra.

Objective Functions

Learning for Spatial & Spectral Consistency

DeepSRM-AI uses a multi-objective loss function to enforce physical radiometry and sharp boundary alignment.

Purpose: Image Quality

Reconstruction Loss

Minimizes pixel-level mean squared error (MSE) and structural dissimilarity between reconstructed and reference surface reflectance.

Purpose: Land-Cover Accuracy

Semantic Loss

Uses categorical cross-entropy and Focal Loss to optimize sub-pixel categorical land-cover classifications.

Purpose: Spectral Preservation

Spectral Consistency Loss

Enforces Spectral Angle Mapper (SAM) constraints across all 13 bands to prevent spectral distortion of vegetation indices.

Purpose: Sharp Boundaries

Boundary / Edge Loss

Applies Sobel gradient constraints to sharpen structural transitions along roads, building footprints, and water margins.

Purpose: Geographic Alignment

Spatial Consistency Loss

Ensures sub-pixel reconstructed features conform strictly to projected spatial reference coordinate systems (CRS).

Key Differentiators

What Makes DeepSRM-AI Different?

Novel architectural enhancements designed specifically for Earth observation and remote sensing workflows.

FEATURE 01

Direct Super Resolution

Performs simultaneous spatial resolution enhancement and semantic land-cover prediction in a single unified neural forward pass, eliminating error accumulation.

FEATURE 02

Subpixel Mapping

Estimates sub-pixel land-cover class proportions and spatial arrangements within individual 10m coarse Sentinel-2 pixels.

FEATURE 03

Multi-task Learning

Jointly optimizes multispectral surface reflectance reconstruction alongside categorical land-cover segmentation using shared feature representations.

FEATURE 04

Spectral & Geographic Consistency

Incorporates domain-specific loss functions that preserve physical radiometry across all 13 Sentinel-2 bands while maintaining spatial alignment.

FEATURE 05

Uncertainty-Aware Output

Generates pixel-level confidence maps to explicitly highlight model-inferred details requiring secondary expert verification.

Value Proposition

From Satellite Pixels to Actionable Maps

DeepSRM-AI makes freely available Sentinel-2 imagery more useful for detailed mapping and decision-making.

Agriculture

Delineates micro-field boundaries and supports localized precision farming insights without requiring expensive aerial surveillance.

Urban Planning

Enables high-frequency building footprint and municipal infrastructure tracking from free satellite revisits.

Disaster Management

Provides rapid localized flood surface mapping and emergency evacuation route accessibility checks.

Environment

Monitors forest fragmentation edges, coastal wetland dynamics, and water-body surface shrinkage over time.

Economic Value

Reduces dependence on costly high-resolution imagery and manual analysis.
